Drop in an image or a 3D scan and watch it get built as a brick sculpture, one brick at a time.

Scan a real object with your iPhone
  1. Install a 3D scanning app, such as Scaniverse, Polycam, or KIRI Engine. Or use Apple's Object Capture in Reality Composer.
  2. Put the object somewhere with even light and walk slowly all the way around it. Get the top too, and keep the whole object in view.
  3. Process the scan as a mesh (not a splat), crop away the floor, and export as GLB. OBJ works too if you choose its .mtl and texture files along with it.
  4. Open this page on the iPhone and choose the file from Files, or AirDrop it to your computer and drop it here.

Apple's own USDZ exports use a binary format browsers can't read yet, so export GLB or OBJ when the app offers it.

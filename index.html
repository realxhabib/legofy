Tips for a good scan
  1. Put the object on a table in even light, against a background that looks different from it.
  2. Tap Scan a real object, allow camera and motion access, then tap the object on screen.
  3. Walk slowly all the way around it, keeping the whole object in view. Then do a second, lower lap from the side (crouch for things on the floor): views from above can't see how tall it is.
  4. Keep the object filling a good part of the screen, and move slowly so frames aren't blurry.
  5. Has an opening (a shoe, a cup)? Include a few shots looking down into it, so the on-device depth model can see how deep it goes.
  6. Tap Done, check the 3D preview, and hide any ⚠ captures that make it worse. Everything happens on your phone, and nothing is uploaded.
